The Destination

Location

Playa Flamingo

Costa Rica's
Gold Coast

Nestled on the coveted Gold Coast of Guanacaste, Playa Flamingo is renowned for its pristine white-sand beach, crystal-clear waters, and world-class sunsets. Casa Nina sits just steps from the shore, with everything you need within reach.

Sixty minutes from Liberia International (LIR), roughly five hours from San José, and within easy day-trip distance of Rincon de la Vieja volcano, Arenal, and Manuel Antonio. The Guanacaste dry season (December–April) brings reliable sunshine and the region's signature long, golden afternoons.

Nearby Beaches

White Sand & Crystal Waters

Some of Costa Rica's most beautiful beaches are within minutes of Casa Nina.

Playa Flamingo

One of the few white-sand beaches in Costa Rica. Calm waters perfect for swimming, paddleboarding, and snorkeling. Walking distance from the villa.

Playa Conchal

A stunning shell beach with turquoise waters, just 10 minutes away. Often rated one of the best beaches in Central America.

Tamarindo

A vibrant surf town 25 minutes south. World-class waves, lively nightlife, excellent restaurants, and boutique shopping.
