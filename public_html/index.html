Welcome to Casa Nina

Bring everyone. There is room.

Casa Nina Flamingo is a newly built three-storey villa above Playa Flamingo on Costa Rica’s Guanacaste coast, with seven bedrooms, six bathrooms and space for fourteen. The first floor is a complete home of its own; the second and third floors hold the main residence and the rooftop terrace, and a private elevator connects all three. Grandparents, parents and kids get to be together without being on top of each other.

The beach is a short walk downhill, the marina a few minutes further, and Liberia airport about an hour by road. Private airport transfers, daily housekeeping, two chef-cooked breakfasts and our concierge are included with every stay, so the holiday starts the moment you land.

Made for gathering
