Location & Good to Know

Welcome to the Gold Coast

First trip to Guanacaste? Here's the short version.

Playa Flamingo destination guide

Playa Flamingo sits on Costa Rica's Gold Coast in Guanacaste province: a white-sand beach town on the northern Pacific, about an hour from Liberia International Airport (LIR).

Flamingo pairs a genuinely beautiful beach with everything close at hand: restaurants, a marina, and day trips in every direction.

Dry season runs roughly December through April, with reliable sun and long blue days. Green season, May through November, trades a brief afternoon shower for lush green hills, fewer crowds and gentler rates. Many of our guests call it their favorite secret.

There is no bad month here, only different moods.

Costa Rica uses the colón, but US dollars are accepted almost everywhere in Guanacaste and cards are widely taken.

Restaurants add a 10% service charge by law. Rounding up for great service, and for your guides and drivers, is appreciated but never expected.

Costa Rican tap water is generally safe to drink, and bottled water is easy to stock through our grocery delivery if you prefer it.

Spanish is the local language, English is spoken widely across the area, and our team is happy to host you in English every day.
