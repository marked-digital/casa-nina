Before you book

Good to know

Two ways to reserve

Send an inquiry below and a member of our team replies personally, or book instantly on Airbnb. Booking direct is always where you will find our best rates.

Minimum stay

Four nights. Over Christmas, New Year’s, March Break and Easter the minimum is seven nights.

Deposit and balance

A 50% deposit secures your dates and the balance is due 14 days before arrival, by credit card. Both are set out in your booking summary before you pay.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 10:00 AM. Our concierge coordinates your exact arrival over WhatsApp.

Getting here

Casa Nina is about an hour by road from Liberia International Airport (LIR). Private pick-up and drop-off are included with every stay.

The Half Casa is private

A three-bedroom home on the first floor with its own kitchen, living space and entrance. When you reserve it, the rest of the villa is never rented to anyone else.

Cancellation terms, the refundable damage hold, early check-in and everything else about a stay are answered in full on our FAQ.
