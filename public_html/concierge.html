Concierge &
Experiences

Your Trip, Curated

We Handle
Every Detail

From your private chef to your fishing charter, every detail of your stay can be arranged before you arrive. Our concierge team handles the logistics so you can focus on the trip itself.

🍴

Private Chef

Professional chefs prepare gourmet meals in your villa using fresh local ingredients. From traditional Costa Rican cuisine to international dishes, tailored to your preferences.

🧹

Cleaning & Turnover

Professional housekeeping and turnover service ensures your villa is spotless throughout your stay. Daily cleaning available on request.

✈️

Airport Transfers

Private transportation to and from Liberia International Airport (LIR). Comfortable, air-conditioned vehicles with bilingual drivers. 60-minute scenic drive.

💆

Spa & Massage

In-villa spa treatments and massage services. Professional therapists bring the spa to you — poolside, on the rooftop, or in the comfort of your room.

🛒

Grocery Stocking

Arrive to a fully stocked kitchen. Share your grocery list and preferences in advance, and everything will be waiting for you when you walk through the door.

🚗

Golf Cart & Car Rentals

Explore Flamingo and surrounding beaches at your own pace. Golf carts are the local way to get around, and car rentals are available for longer excursions.

🎣

Fishing Charters

World-class sport fishing out of Marina Flamingo. Full and half-day charters targeting marlin, sailfish, mahi-mahi, tuna, and roosterfish year-round.

⛵

Catamaran Tours

Sunset sailing, snorkeling excursions, and private catamaran charters along the stunning Guanacaste coastline. Includes drinks, snacks, and snorkeling gear.

🏄

Surf Lessons

Beginner to advanced surf instruction at nearby breaks. Professional instructors provide boards, rash guards, and expert coaching in warm Pacific waters.

🌳

Ziplining

Soar through the tropical canopy on world-class zipline courses. An adrenaline-pumping way to experience Costa Rica's incredible biodiversity from above.

🐎

Horseback Riding

Ride along the beach at sunset or through mountain trails with stunning ocean views. Guided tours suitable for all experience levels.

🏔

ATV Tours

Off-road adventures through Costa Rica's backcountry, river crossings, and mountain trails. Guided tours with top-quality equipment and safety gear.

🚢

Jet Skis

High-speed fun on the Pacific. Jet ski rentals available at nearby beaches with guided tours along the coast for those who want a local's perspective.
