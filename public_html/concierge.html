Concierge

Every detail, arranged before you land

From your private chef to your fishing charter, every part of your stay at Casa Nina Flamingo can be arranged before you arrive. Our concierge team in Playa Flamingo handles the logistics, so you can focus on the trip itself.

Scroll

Included with every stay

These are not extras

Four things every group gets without asking. Everything further down this page is arranged on request.

Dedicated concierge

One point of contact from booking to checkout, on WhatsApp whenever you need them.

Private airport transfers

Pick-up and drop-off at Liberia International (LIR), about an hour away, in air-conditioned vehicles with bilingual drivers.

Daily housekeeping

The villa is refreshed every morning by our housekeeping team, so the beds are made, the bathrooms refreshed and the kitchen is clean.

Two chef breakfasts

Our private chef cooks breakfast for the whole group on two mornings of your stay. You pay only for the food.

At the villa

Brought to your door

Private chef

Beyond the included breakfasts, our chef cooks lunches and dinners in your kitchen with fresh local ingredients: Costa Rican classics, or a menu built around your group. You choose the dishes.

Spa & massage

Massages and treatments with professional therapists who come to you: by the pool, on the rooftop, or in your room.

Grocery stocking

Arrive to a full fridge. Send your list ahead and everything is put away before you land.

Golf carts & cars

Golf carts (4 or 6 passenger) are how Flamingo gets around, and they are welcome on all the local roads. For longer days out, rental cars in all different sizes are a message away.

On the water

Out of Marina Flamingo

Fishing charters

Sport fishing out of Marina Flamingo, a few minutes from the villa. Full and half-day charters for marlin, sailfish, mahi-mahi, tuna and roosterfish, year-round. Bring dinner back to the kitchen.

Catamaran tours

Sunset sails, snorkelling stops and private catamaran charters along the Guanacaste coast. Drinks, snacks and snorkelling gear are on board.

Surf lessons

Lessons for first-timers through to advanced surfers at the nearby breaks. Boards, rash guards and coaching are provided, and the water is warm enough to skip the wetsuit.

Jet skis

Rentals at the nearby beaches, with guided rides along the coast if you want someone who knows the coves.

Explore the water around Playa Flamingo

On land

Into the hills

Ziplining

Zipline courses through the forest canopy, with guides and gear taken care of. The quickest way to meet the trees, and the monkeys in them.

Horseback riding

The beach at sunset, or hill trails with the ocean below. Guided rides for every level, first-timers included.

ATV tours

Off-road through the backcountry, river crossings and hill trails, with guides, machines and safety gear included.

Day trips

The volcano and hot springs at Rincón de la Vieja, a river safari through Palo Verde, or the waterfall at Llanos de Cortés. Leave after breakfast and be back for sunset, with the guide and the transport arranged.
