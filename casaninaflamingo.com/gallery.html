Exterior & arrival

Three white floors on the hillside, a gated drive with parking inside, and a step-free ramp from the driveway to the front door. This is what you pull up to.

Pool & terraces

The private infinity pool faces the bay, with a covered patio and a live-edge table for dinner outside, loungers for the afternoon, and a terrace on every floor up to the rooftop.

Living rooms

Two living rooms, one on each main floor, with doors that fold away to the terraces. Somewhere for the film and somewhere for the card game.

Kitchens & dining

Two fully equipped kitchens, one on each main floor, each with an island and bar seating and a live-edge table for the whole group. Cook together, cook apart, or hand it all to our chef.

11 photos

05

Bedrooms

Seven bedrooms, five with king beds and two with queens, all with air conditioning, blackout curtains and a view. Two of them have walk-in closets; the top-floor suite has the whole roof to itself.

06

Bathrooms & laundry

Five full bathrooms with walk-in showers and marble finishes, and two powder rooms, one of them right by the pool so the sand stays outside. A washer and dryer on each of the main floors, so nobody flies home with a suitcase of laundry.

Rooftop & views

The rooftop terrace at golden hour, with the whole group up there for it, and the view over Playa Flamingo bay from every floor.
